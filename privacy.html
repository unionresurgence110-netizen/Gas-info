Privacy Policy

Your information matters. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through GAS-INFO may be collected, used, retained and managed when you use our gas availability information platform.

🛡️ Your Privacy Matters

📋

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, GAS-INFO may collect information submitted through searches, registration forms, account settings, reviews, ratings, reports and trader stock updates.

Information collected will depend on the functionality you use and the information you choose to submit.

👤

2. Account Information

When you register, information requested through the registration process may be used to create and manage your account and provide account-related features.

Users should provide accurate information and avoid submitting unnecessary personal details.

📍

3. Location and Search Information

You may enter location details such as province, district, municipality, ward or other location information to search for gas cylinder availability.

Search details may be used to identify relevant trader-provided availability information.

⭐

4. Reviews, Ratings and Reports

Reviews, ratings and reports may be processed to display user feedback, investigate reported issues, identify potentially misleading information and maintain platform reliability.

Relevant report information may be retained while a report is under review or while necessary follow-up action is being completed.

🏪

5. Trader and Stock Information

Trader-provided information may include shop details, location information and gas cylinder stock availability submitted through the platform.

GAS-INFO is an information platform. It does not itself sell gas cylinders, process gas purchases or provide gas delivery.

Stock information may change at any time. Customers should verify availability directly with the relevant trader.

🔐

6. Data Protection and Use

Information may be used to operate and maintain GAS-INFO, provide account features, display relevant results, manage reviews, investigate reports and protect the platform against misuse.

GAS-INFO aims to apply appropriate safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss. Actual safeguards depend on the platform's implemented security measures.

📝

7. Information Retention and Your Choices

GAS-INFO retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the platform, provide its features, maintain security, review reports, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal requirements.

Information associated with a report may be retained while the report is being reviewed and any necessary moderation or follow-up action is being completed. Afterward, the information will be handled according to the applicable retention and deletion procedures.

Logging Out

Logging out ends your current account session. It does not, by itself, delete your account or the information associated with it.

You can sign in again using your existing account, subject to the platform's account and security requirements.

Account and Data Deletion

Users can choose account and data deletion separately from a normal logout. Before confirming deletion, users should be informed about its consequences and the categories of information affected.

Permanent account deletion removes the account and associated personal information covered by the deletion process. Information that is not eligible for deletion may be retained where there is a valid legal, security or dispute-related reason.

After an account has been permanently deleted, you may need to register again to use account-based features. Creating a new account does not necessarily restore information from the deleted account.

Where applicable, users may contact GAS-INFO to request access to or correction of their personal information. Requests may require identity verification.

Reports and Necessary Records

Deleting an account does not necessarily mean that every report, moderation record or security record will be immediately deleted. Limited information may be retained when necessary to complete an unresolved review, address misuse, resolve a dispute or comply with applicable law. Such information should be retained only for the relevant purpose and period.

🔄

8. Policy Updates

GAS-INFO may update this Privacy Policy when platform features, information collection practices, data handling procedures or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version of this policy will be published on this page. The policy should accurately describe the information handling practices and controls in effect.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically to stay informed about how GAS-INFO handles information. Where required, users will be notified of significant changes through an appropriate method.

💬
