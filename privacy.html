Privacy Policy

Learn how GAS-INFO handles information on its platform.

GAS-INFO is an information platform that helps customers discover trader-provided gas cylinder availability information. GAS-INFO does not itself sell gas cylinders or guarantee stock availability.

Information and Platform Use

Information submitted through searches, reviews, ratings, reports, or account forms may be used to provide and maintain platform features. The exact information collected depends on the features you use.

Accuracy of Stock Information

Stock information is provided by traders and may change at any time. Users should verify availability directly with the relevant trader.

Reviews and Reports

Reviews, ratings, and reports may be processed to help maintain the reliability of platform information and address reported issues.

Questions About Privacy

Please use the GAS-INFO contact page for privacy-related questions.
