About GAS-INFO

Connecting customers with trader-provided gas availability information.

What is GAS-INFO?

GAS-INFO is a Nepal-focused information platform designed to help customers find information about gas cylinder availability reported by gas traders.

The platform connects customers and traders by making availability information easier to discover.

Why was GAS-INFO created?

Finding a gas cylinder can sometimes be difficult when people do not know which nearby trader may have stock.

GAS-INFO was created to make this information easier to find through location-based searching and trader-provided stock updates.

The Problem We Are Solving

Customers may have to visit or contact multiple shops to find out whether a particular gas cylinder is available.

GAS-INFO aims to reduce this unnecessary searching by bringing available trader information into one place.

How GAS-INFO Works

  • Customers search using location, cylinder and quantity information.
  • Available location choices are based on information present in the GAS-INFO system.
  • Traders register and provide their shop information.
  • Traders update their own stock information.
  • Search results can consider stock suitability, rating and freshness of the information.

For Customers

Customers can use GAS-INFO to search for trader information without needing to purchase or book gas through the platform.

  • Search for gas availability information.
  • Explore available trader information.
  • View ratings and reviews.
  • Report incorrect information.

For Traders

Traders can register and provide information about their shop and available stock.

  • Add shop information.
  • Update cylinder stock.
  • Maintain their stock information.
  • Receive relevant notifications and reports.
  • Manage their trader account.

What GAS-INFO Does NOT Do

GAS-INFO is an information platform only.

  • It does not sell gas.
  • It does not process gas purchases.
  • It does not provide booking or reservations.
  • It does not provide gas delivery.
  • It does not process payments.

Information Accuracy

Stock and shop information is provided by traders and may change at any time.

GAS-INFO provides tools for reporting incorrect information, but trader-provided information should not automatically be considered verified.

Stock Availability Disclaimer

Stock information is provided by the trader and may change at any time. Please check the latest information before visiting a shop.

Limitation of Responsibility

GAS-INFO is not responsible for transactions, delivery, gas quality, price, fraud, or other losses resulting from interactions between customers and traders.

Trust & Transparency

GAS-INFO aims to maintain transparency by showing information about stock freshness, ratings, reviews and reporting systems.

Information that violates the platform rules may be reviewed and removed according to the applicable moderation process.

Future Vision

GAS-INFO aims to improve the way customers discover gas availability information across Nepal while giving traders useful tools to maintain accurate information.

Disclaimer

GAS-INFO provides information based on data submitted through the platform. Availability information may become outdated after a trader's update.

Users should verify the latest information before visiting a trader. GAS-INFO does not guarantee that reported stock will remain available.
