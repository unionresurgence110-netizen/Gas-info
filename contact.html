How Reports Work

Reports are intended to help GAS-INFO identify potentially incorrect or problematic information.

1. Submit a Report A logged-in customer can submit a supported report about relevant information.
2. Review The report may be reviewed before any action is taken.
3. Trader Verification Where appropriate, the trader may be asked to verify the reported information.
4. Resolution GAS-INFO may update, correct, moderate, or otherwise handle the information according to its applicable rules.

Login Required for Reports

Reporting features that require account tracking will be available to logged-in customers.

Why login?

Login helps GAS-INFO associate reports with an account, reduce misuse, and support the review and moderation process.

Important Reminder

A report does not automatically mean that the reported information is incorrect.

GAS-INFO may need to review or verify information before taking action.
