About Ratings & Reviews

GAS-INFO uses reviews as a way for users to share useful experiences while keeping the platform focused on gas availability information.

  • Ratings and reviews are user-generated information.
  • GAS-INFO does not create or invent customer reviews.
  • Reviews may be moderated when they violate the platform's rules.
  • Reviews do not guarantee the current availability of gas at a shop.
  • Stock information is provided by traders and may change after an update.
