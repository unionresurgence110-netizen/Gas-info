Legal & Compliance

Important legal and compliance information relating to the use of the GAS-INFO platform.

1. Nature of GAS-INFO

GAS-INFO is an information platform designed to help users discover gas availability information provided by registered traders.

GAS-INFO itself does not sell gas cylinders, purchase gas cylinders, arrange delivery, process payments, or act as a booking or reservation service.

2. Trader-Provided Information

Information about shops and gas stock may be submitted and updated by traders using the platform.

GAS-INFO does not guarantee that trader-provided information will always be complete, accurate, current, or available.

Important Stock Disclaimer

Stock information is provided by the trader and may change at any time. Please check the latest information before visiting a shop.

3. No Guarantee of Availability

Information displayed by GAS-INFO should not be treated as a guarantee that a gas cylinder will be available when a user visits a shop.

Stock can change between the time a trader updates the platform and the time a customer reaches the shop.

4. Independent Traders

Traders listed on GAS-INFO are independent businesses. GAS-INFO does not automatically represent that a trader is an employee, agent, partner, or representative of GAS-INFO.

Information about a trader's business, shop, stock, or services is provided for informational purposes.

5. User-Generated Content

Ratings, reviews, reports, and other supported user-submitted information may be displayed on the platform according to GAS-INFO's systems and rules.

  • Users should submit truthful and relevant information.
  • Users should not intentionally submit misleading content.
  • GAS-INFO may moderate or remove content that violates platform rules.

6. Reports & Information Verification

GAS-INFO may provide reporting and verification mechanisms for certain information issues.

Submitting a report does not automatically mean that the reported information is incorrect. Reports may require review or verification before action is taken.

7. Privacy & Account Information

Account and personal information is handled according to the GAS-INFO privacy practices and applicable requirements.

Users should review the applicable privacy information before creating or using an account.

GAS-INFO does not ask customers to provide unnecessary information simply to search for gas availability.

8. Platform Security

However, no online platform can guarantee absolute security or uninterrupted availability.

11. Limitation of Responsibility

Users are responsible for making their own decisions based on the information available to them.

Before Visiting a Shop

Always consider checking the latest available information directly before travelling to a shop.
